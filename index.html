Daily Brief

Daily Brief is a personal tool that runs on one person's Mac and writes them a morning and evening summary of their own email, calendars, and to-dos. It is not a public service and has no other users.

It signs in to the owner's own Google accounts with OAuth and uses:

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): to find emails that need a reply. It never sends, deletes, archives, or changes mail.
  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly): to show the day's schedule.
  • Calendar events on calendars the owner owns (calendar.events.owned): only to set the owner's own RSVP on an invite, or to copy an event onto the owner's calendar, when the owner clicks a button.
